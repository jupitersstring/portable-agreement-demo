Beyond this example

This demo isolates the commitment record. The broader developer protocol has additional, separately configured building blocks:

CapabilityWhat it addsWhat still needs an explicit choice
Authority and delegationOwner limits, role signatures, scoped delegation and approval of exact rendered terms.Owner keys, protected roles and an adopted approval method. This tab simulates both parties.
Complex agreementsMilestones, linked subcontracts, guarantors, typed obligations and reusable transaction patterns.Each party's precise meaning, dependencies and assent. Prose is not automatically executed.
Payments and marketsOptional escrow, credit and marketplace flows through adopted settlement services and adapters.A real provider, payment evidence and separately authorised effects. The demo has none.
DisputesAgreed procedures, arbitration stages, appeals, custody chains and signed awards.Appointed decision-makers and the exact agreed remedy. An award is not automatic legal recognition.
Privacy and proofsEncrypted carriers, scoped disclosure and proof envelopes for adopted verifiers.Keys, the proved statement and verifier assumptions. Public disclosures cannot be taken back.
Discovery and reputationSigned descriptors, contextual receipt evidence and bounded graph ranking.Trust anchors and context. Signatures and scores do not establish truth or independent control.
Offline and other systemsFile/QR/store-and-forward records, MCP/A2A adapters and optional blockchain anchoring.Real carrier and operator deployment. An offline signature cannot prevent a double spend.

Developer-stage capabilities are not a production-security, legal, payment-provider or physical-radio certification. The browser verifier covers the demonstrated profile with strict WebCrypto Ed25519 verification; it does not replace every historical SDK verifier.

Private by locality: there is no telemetry, local storage, cookies, external font or network API. Downloading or sharing a record is a separate disclosure. Closing this tab discards its signing keys. Exports always contain the exact terms, public keys, signatures and event hashes, plus evidence text if explicitly included. They contain no private signing keys. Hashes are not encryption.
