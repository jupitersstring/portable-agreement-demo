Technical details

The browser checks this demo's portable work-order /0.3 records with WebCrypto Ed25519. It does not check every format used by the wider product. A signature checks approval of a version; it does not prove real identity, work quality, payment or legal effect. A changed version does not automatically cancel duties under an earlier one.

The broader product is still in development. This demo does not provide a payment service, arbitration service, hardware safety certification, Bluetooth or radio connection, or a production blockchain.

This app has no accounts or analytics. Closing the tab discards its signing keys, so save a record you want to keep. The file is not encrypted: share it only with the people you choose.
