Why keep the agreement outside any one agent?

Work can pass through several people, agents and services. A shared record helps the next participant see the permission, the exact terms and the approvals already given. You can change tools without losing the agreement's history.

Covenant's wider early-access product is being developed for these kinds of coordinated workflows. These small local examples make the approval steps visible.
